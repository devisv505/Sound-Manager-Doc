These pictures come from the running Unity demos and their graph editors. The **Game view** shows what the player sees, including the real on-screen controls. The **graph view** shows the connected blocks that decide which sounds play.

Choose a demo to compare its states. Select any picture to enlarge it; graph labels are easiest to read at full size. Each picture also has steps you can follow in Unity.
