**08 / A little groove.** Play a record, change tracks, and pause. The demo starts a second music play for a transition and changes the two gains over time. The dancer’s speed comes from a BPM value stored for each track.

These are real Unity captures with the scene’s own models, camera, and controls. Select a picture to enlarge it, or open **Try this in Unity** below it for the steps. The graph close views explain individual parts of the full graph.
