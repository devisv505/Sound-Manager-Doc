Reviewed 20 September 2026 against Unity source revision `746a1cc7d2602bc59df6096eac50508ded6ef49e` (Unity 6000.6.0f1). This is a local release-readiness review. Publication and verification of the hosted revision remain M7.

The reading review followed the first-sound → setup/keys → first graph → parameters/position/stop/results path, reviewed the introductions and navigation across all reference families, checked all ten demo walkthroughs and their graph/controller boundaries, and checked recipe setup, component placement, expected behavior, and cleanup. The explicit limitations are described on their relevant pages.
