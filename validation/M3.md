## Authenticity and source checks

Game View PNGs read Unity's composed render surface at 1920 × 1080, including the real HUD, camera, post-processing, models, and effects. Graphs and Inspectors read their native GUI surfaces at the captured dimensions. Only Metal's vertical texture-row origin is normalized. No screenshot was generated, repainted, relabeled, or assembled from separate UI pieces.

The helpers call the same scene controllers used by the HUD; Footstep uses a temporary Input System keyboard to walk naturally through all three lanes. The manifest records the actual controller values and source file hashes. Relevant graph importer `.meta` files are hashed because playback settings are stored there. Sixty referenced source files still match their recorded hashes, and the Unity source revision/worktree was checked again after capture.

The 18 capture-state/source checks in [capture-checks.json](m3/capture-checks.json) pass. They confirm the required transient states, including an active lightning bolt at about 0.08 seconds, simultaneous working-saw/hammer-tap state with two voices, nonzero incoming/outgoing music gains, a detached echo alive after owner loss and a Stop echo no longer alive, and a paused rocket at its captured flight altitude.

Image readback and the HUD's refresh are not guaranteed to occur on the same update. The lightning HUD still shows the previous thunder count during its first flash; the caption explains this. Arcade's busy frame shows accepted/replaced/limited results, while cooldown remains zero. Captions do not claim every counter increased. Launch's large preparing-phase label remains “Ready for the sky” during its smaller pressure-warning status. These are actual demo states, not edited labels. Pictures and state records do not establish subjective sound quality or DSP timing guarantees.
